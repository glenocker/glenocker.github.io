ABOUT THIS COLLECTION

From symptom to working repair.

These four repairs document how I diagnosed engine and fuel-system issues, used measurements or mechanical inspection to choose a repair, and completed service procedures on my own and family dirt bikes. Each entry describes the problem, likely cause, work performed, and evidence available.

REPAIR 01 / ENGINE & VALVETRAIN

Top-End Rebuild on 2017 Honda CRF250R.

Repair goal

Restore normal starting and idle behavior by addressing the valve-clearance problem and refreshing worn top-end components.

Issue

Intake and exhaust valve clearance was going out of specification quicker than normal, leaving the bike hard to start and more inclined to stall when idling.

Reasoning

This made sense because the valves had a high number of hours on them and were due for replacement. The old valves began to tulip or deform, allowing too much air to escape when the valves were supposed to be shut, which reduced cylinder compression.

Solution

I installed new valves in the cylinder head and then readjusted the valve clearances. While the top end of the engine was open, I also took the cylinder to a local machine shop to be honed, and I installed a new piston, piston rings, and new head gaskets.

Work completed / evidence

Replaced valves, re-shimmed the valve train, installed a new piston/rings/gaskets, and had the cylinder honed. Photos show disassembly and the reassembled bike; compression measurements are not included.

REPAIR 02 / VALVETRAIN SERVICE

Valve Adjustments.

Repair goal

Return intake and exhaust valve clearance to the manufacturer’s specified range.

Issue

Intake and exhaust valve clearance was going out of spec, leaving the bike hard to start and more inclined to stall when idling.

Reasoning

This is normal to see after enough operating hours because valves slowly wear and the clearance changes over time. Periodically checking valve lash is important to keep the engine within the manufacturer's service specifications.

Solution

I opened the cylinder-head cover, set the engine to top dead center, and measured valve clearances with a feeler gauge. After that, I removed the cam chain, camshaft, and valve lifters, selected new shims based on the measured and target clearance values, and then reassembled the valve train and measured clearance again to confirm everything was back within specification.

Work completed / evidence

Set the engine at the specified top-dead-center position, measured with feeler gauges, adjusted shim sizes, reassembled, and checked clearance again. No numeric before/after log is shown.

REPAIR 03 / FUEL SYSTEM

Carburetor Rebuild on 2005 Honda CRF450.

Repair goal

Correct a no-start condition consistent with restricted fuel passages after gasoline sat in the carburetor.

Issue

The bike would not start, even though it sounded like it wanted to fire.

Reasoning

Fuel had been left sitting in the carburetor for too long, which likely clogged the jets and internal passages inside the carburetor.

Solution

I removed the carburetor, tore it apart, and cleaned all the jets and passages with carburetor cleaner and compressed air. After cleaning, I reassembled everything with fresh seals and gaskets and then reinstalled the carburetor on the bike.

Work completed / evidence

Removed, disassembled, cleaned, and reassembled the carburetor with new seals. Photos document the carb body and jets before installation; no measured flow or engine-running test is included.

REPAIR 04 / DRIVETRAIN

Clutch Replacement on 2025 Yamaha YZ250F.

Repair goal

Investigate clutch slip at higher RPM on a nearly new YZ250F.

Issue

The clutch was slipping under higher RPM and not transferring power to the gearbox correctly. It was an unusual problem to have on a new bike with only about 20 total hours on the engine.

Reasoning

The rider was on the clutch a lot and not especially forgiving on it. That caused the friction plates in the clutch pack to wear down quicker than normal and begin slipping under load.

Solution

I replaced the clutch friction plates and steel plates with new components. I also worked through the diagnosis by checking the clutch pack and springs, along with related fluid service.

Work completed / evidence

Replaced the friction and steel clutch plates; spring-pack and oil-service troubleshooting are also documented. No photos or quantitative post-repair slip data are available.
